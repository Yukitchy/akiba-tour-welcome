01
Figures, cards, hobby shops

RADIO KAIKAN

10:00–20:00

The ten-floor landmark right outside the Electric Town exit. Floors of figures, trading cards, dolls and model kits — if it exists in Akihabara, it is probably somewhere in this building.

02
Second-hand manga & toys

MANDARAKE COMPLEX

12:00–20:00

Eight floors of pre-owned manga, doujinshi, toys, animation cels and cards. This is the place to hunt for the rare thing that sold out everywhere else years ago.

03
New releases & goods

ANIMATE AKIHABARA

10:00–21:00

Animate’s Akihabara flagship, renewed in 2026: new manga, anime discs, music and character goods across two buildings, with event floors for whatever is airing now.

04
Game centre

SILK HAT AKIHABARA

10:00–24:00

The famous red arcade tower right by the station — crane games, rhythm games and fighters over eight floors. It was SEGA, then GiGO, for over thirty years; GiGO’s other Akihabara buildings are a short walk away.

05
Retro games

SUPER POTATO

11:00–20:00

Three floors of Famicom, Mega Drive and PlayStation history, with a retro arcade corner on the top floor. Pure nostalgia — and nearly all of it is playable and buyable.

06
Capsule toys

AKIHABARA GACHAPON KAIKAN

11:00–20:00

Around 500 capsule machines packed into one narrow shop, with new series landing every month (Sundays it closes at 19:00). Bring 100-yen coins — you will want more than one try.
